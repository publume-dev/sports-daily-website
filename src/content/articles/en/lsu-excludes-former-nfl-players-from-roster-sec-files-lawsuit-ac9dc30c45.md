- LSU did not include former NFL players Dae'Quan Wright and Zxavian Harris on its roster for its season opener against Clemson.
- The SEC filed its own lawsuit against LSU and coach Lane Kiffin in federal court on Thursday, seeking to enforce its ban on professional players returning to college football.

The roster exclusion and the lawsuit could influence LSU's roster strength this season and bring the rule against professional players returning to college football into focus.

Watch for the federal court's ruling on the SEC's lawsuit and whether LSU will appeal the roster decision.
