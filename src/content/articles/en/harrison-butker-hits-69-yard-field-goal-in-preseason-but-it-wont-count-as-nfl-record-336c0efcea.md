- Harrison Butker made a 69-yard field goal in a preseason game against the Buccaneers, which would have been an NFL record but does not count because it was preseason.
- The kick is tied for the second-longest field goal in football history, with only Cam Little's 70-yarder in 2025 preseason and Ove Johansson's 69-yarder in 1976 being comparable.
- The NFL's new K-ball rule, implemented last season, allows teams to break in 60 footballs per season, which has contributed to longer field goals and may lead to more record-breaking kicks.
