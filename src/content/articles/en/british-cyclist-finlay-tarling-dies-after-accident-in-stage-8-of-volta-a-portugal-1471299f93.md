- British cyclist Finlay Tarling, 19, died after a serious accident during stage 8 of the Volta a Portugal.
- Stage 8 was neutralized with 23 kilometers remaining, and the podium ceremony was canceled.
- The race organizer, Volta a Portugal em Bicicleta, Jogos Santa Casa, confirmed the death and extended condolences to Tarling's family, team, and friends.
- Tarling was a highly rated young talent, having placed third in the under-23 time trial at the British National Championships this summer.
- The exact circumstances of the accident are not yet confirmed; reports suggest he was hit by a non-race vehicle traveling in the opposite direction, but this is not officially confirmed.
