- Senegal have appointed Patrick Vieira as head coach, succeeding Pape Thiaw, who was dismissed after the World Cup exit.
- The Senegalese football federation (FSF) announced the appointment on Monday, describing it as a 'strategic move' to establish a high-level technical staff in line with the country's sporting ambitions.
- Vieira's main mission will be to revitalise the team after Senegal's loss to Belgium in the last 32 of the World Cup, where they led 2-0 in the 86th minute before losing in extra time.
- Senegal have appealed the decision to strip them of the Africa Cup of Nations title, with a hearing at the Court of Arbitration for Sport in Lausanne scheduled for 8 October.
