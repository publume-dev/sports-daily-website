- Manchester City have agreed a deal to sign Lille midfielder Ayyoub Bouaddi, with the transfer expected to be completed in the coming days.
- The fee is reported as £86m by the BBC, or €95m (£81.4m) plus €5m in add-ons by The Guardian.
- Bouaddi, an 18-year-old Morocco international, started five of Morocco's six games at the 2026 World Cup; he was stood down from Lille's match against Angers on Sunday ahead of the move.
- The majority of the fee comes from the £65m sale of Rodri to Barcelona last week.
