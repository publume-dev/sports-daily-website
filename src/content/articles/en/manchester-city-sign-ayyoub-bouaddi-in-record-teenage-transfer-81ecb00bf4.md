- Manchester City have signed midfielder Ayyoub Bouaddi from Lille in a deal worth £85.6m (€95m plus €5m in add-ons), making the 18-year-old Morocco international the Premier League's most expensive teenager.
- Bouaddi has signed a five-year contract with Manchester City, subject to international clearance.
- The transfer follows the departures of Rodri to Barcelona and Tijjani Reijnders to Al-Qadsiah, and the fee surpasses the previous English record for a teenager paid by Manchester United for Leny Yoro.
- Bouaddi made 96 appearances for Lille, helped them finish third in Ligue 1 last season, and starred in a Champions League victory over Real Madrid.
- Manchester City have agreed to sell Nico González to Newcastle for £52m and loan Omar Marmoush to Tottenham with an obligation to buy for an initial £50m.
- City are expected to make a bid for Chelsea midfielder Enzo Fernández, who is valued at £120m.
