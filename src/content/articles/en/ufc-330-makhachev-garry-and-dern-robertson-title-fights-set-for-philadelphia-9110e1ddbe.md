- UFC 330 takes place Saturday, Aug. 15 at Xfinity Mobile Arena in Philadelphia and streams on Paramount+.
- Islam Makhachev defends the UFC welterweight championship against Ian Machado Garry in the main event.
- Mackenzie Dern makes the first defense of her women's strawweight championship against Gillian Robertson in the co-main event.
- Makhachev won the welterweight title with a five-round decision over Jack Della Maddalena last November.
- Ian Machado Garry, 15-0 before a December 2024 loss to Shavkat Rakhmonov, has rebounded with two wins to earn the title shot.
- Dern beat Virna Jandiroba in October 2025 to win the vacant belt; Robertson enters on a five-fight winning streak.
