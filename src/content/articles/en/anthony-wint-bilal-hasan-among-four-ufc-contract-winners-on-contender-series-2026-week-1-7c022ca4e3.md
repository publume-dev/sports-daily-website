- Dana White's Contender Series 2026 Week 1 awarded four UFC contracts on Aug. 11, 2026.
- Anthony Wint defeated Matt Adams via first-round TKO (punches) in 34 seconds and earned a UFC contract.
- Bilal Hasan defeated Mridul Saikia via first-round TKO (punches) in under one minute and earned a UFC contract.
- Thomas Pagliarulo rallied from early trouble to defeat Ananias Mulumba via third-round TKO (strikes) and earned a UFC contract.
- Abe Alsaghir defeated Fabrizio Escarrega via split decision (27-30, 29-28, 29-28) and earned a UFC contract.
