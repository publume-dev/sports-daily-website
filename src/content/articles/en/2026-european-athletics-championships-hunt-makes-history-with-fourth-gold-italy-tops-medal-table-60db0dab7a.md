- Amy Hunt anchored Great Britain's mixed 4x100m relay to victory in a European record of 39.97 seconds, securing her fourth gold and making her the first athlete to win four golds at a single European Championships.
- Italy topped the final medal table with 10 golds, 6 silvers, and 6 bronzes (22 total), ahead of Great Britain (9 golds, 8 silvers, 2 bronzes) and Germany (6 golds, 10 silvers, 5 bronzes).
- Mondo Duplantis of Sweden won the men's pole vault with a championship record of 6.15m, while Sara Kolak of Croatia won the women's javelin with 62.43m.
