- President Donald Trump wrote on Truth Social that FIFA would be making a mistake if it replaced Gianni Infantino.
- Infantino faces calls to resign after a plan to sell about 20% of a proposed FIFA subsidiary, FIFA Forward Enterprise, to private investors for roughly $4 billion fell apart.
- UEFA, Concacaf and the AFC accused Infantino of a “fundamental breach of trust” and implied they would welcome his resignation before the March presidential vote.
- U.S. Soccer backed Concacaf’s statement and called for “meaningful change that strengthens FIFA’s governance, transparency and accountability,” but did not publicly demand Infantino’s resignation.

FIFA’s presidential election is scheduled for March at the FIFA Congress in Rabat, Morocco, with candidate entry open until Nov. 18. Whether Concacaf president Victor Montagliani enters remains an open question; he is reportedly considering a bid, but it has not been confirmed.
