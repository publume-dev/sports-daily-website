- Andreas Leknessund won stage seven of the 2026 Vuelta a España on 28 August 2026, covering the 149.8km route from Vall d'Alba to Aramón Valdelinares.
- Leknessund's Uno-X Mobility teammate Tobias Johannessen finished second, 34 seconds behind, with Wout van Aert third.
- Tadej Pogacar extended his overall lead to 3 minutes 50 seconds over Enric Mas after gaining 16 seconds, with Primož Roglič a further minute behind in third.
- Stage eight on Saturday is a flat 176.4km route from Pucol to Xeraco.
