- British tight end Seydou Traore has been named to the Miami Dolphins' initial 53-man roster, making him eligible to play from the start of the 2026 NFL season.
- Traore was drafted by the Dolphins in the fifth round of the 2026 NFL Draft and is the first graduate of England's NFL Academy to reach the NFL.
- Traore signed a four-year rookie contract worth $4.8 million (£3.6 million) but still needed to survive the roster cut from 90 or 91 players to 53.
- Northern Irish kicker Charlie Smyth has been released by the New Orleans Saints after making his NFL debut last season.
