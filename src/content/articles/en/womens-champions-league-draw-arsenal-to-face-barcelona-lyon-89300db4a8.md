- **Arsenal** will face Barcelona and Lyon in the league phase, hosting Lyon, Benfica, and HB Koge, and traveling to Barcelona, Paris FC, and Inter Milan.
- **Chelsea** will travel to Lyon and host Paris St-Germain, Roma, and Austria Wien, with away trips to Hacken and OH Leuven.
- **Manchester City** will face Bayern Munich, PSG, and Inter Milan away, and host PSG, Real Madrid, and Austria Wien.
- The league phase first matchday is scheduled for 22/23 September 2026, with the final matchday on 16 December 2026, and the final in Warsaw in the final week of May 2027.

The full match schedule for each team will be confirmed by UEFA on Saturday, 5 September.
