- Kate Cross will retire from cricket at the end of the summer.
- She played 102 times for England between 2013 and 2025 and has been playing domestic cricket since losing her England contract last year.
- She will play the remainder of the season for Lancashire, including against The Blaze on Sunday in Nottingham.
- She finishes with 25 Test wickets, 101 in ODIs, and 14 in T20s.
