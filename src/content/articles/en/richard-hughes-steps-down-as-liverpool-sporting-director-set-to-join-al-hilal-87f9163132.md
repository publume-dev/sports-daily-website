- **Departure and destination**: Liverpool sporting director Richard Hughes has stepped down and is set to join Saudi club Al-Hilal.
- **Tenure at Liverpool**: Hughes had been at Liverpool since June 2024, and his departure was expected.
- **Original contract**: Hughes' original contract was set to expire in June 2027.

Watch: The exact start date of Hughes' new role at Al-Hilal has not been specified, which could affect the timeline of Liverpool's search for a new sporting director.
