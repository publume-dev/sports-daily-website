- According to a source with knowledge of the discussions, UEFA, Concacaf and the AFC are drawing up a policy document on how FIFA could be restructured and how its funds should be distributed; the framework's exact contents and the identities of officials involved have not been published.
- The three confederations have begun preliminary discussions about organising new competitions if the standoff with FIFA's leadership persists.
- UEFA says its members will boycott next month's Under-20 Women's World Cup unless FIFA sets up an independent review of the plan to sell 21% of the World Cup and gives legally binding assurances that it will not be repeated.
- Concacaf president Victor Montagliani is seen as the most likely presidential challenger to FIFA president Gianni Infantino at this stage.

Watch: FIFA's response and whether European teams ultimately take part in the Under-20 Women's World Cup are not yet known.
