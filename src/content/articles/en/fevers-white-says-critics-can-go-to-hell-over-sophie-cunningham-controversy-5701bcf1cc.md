- According to The Guardian and CBS Sports, Indiana Fever coach Stephanie White defended her handling of DiJonai Carrington’s flagrant foul on Sophie Cunningham, said she did not see it in real time, called it “egregious” after watching replays, and told critics “everybody else can go to hell.”
- CBS Sports reported that Caitlin Clark and Sophie Cunningham both publicly supported White, with Clark saying the team would win for her and Cunningham saying “me and Steph are great.”
- CBS Sports reported on the Fever’s 106-92 win over the New York Liberty: Clark had 22 points and 10 assists, and Kelsey Mitchell scored 28 points to extend her record 20-point streak to 17 games.
- The Guardian and CBS Sports reported that Indiana improved to 21-12, hold fourth place in the WNBA, and have won nine of their last 12 games.
- The sources conflict on timing: The Guardian says White’s comments came after the game, while CBS Sports says they were made in a pregame media session.
