- UMass beat Rutgers 37-21 on Thursday, ending a 16-game losing streak against FBS opponents, the longest active such streak in the nation.
- Rutgers entered the game as a 29.5-point favorite; FBS teams favored by 29-plus points had been 213-0 since 2024.
- UMass quarterback Pop Watson completed 18 of 21 passes for 189 yards and three touchdowns, adding 40 rushing yards.
- UMass's defense recorded seven tackles for loss, five sacks, and forced four turnovers, winning the turnover battle 4-0.
