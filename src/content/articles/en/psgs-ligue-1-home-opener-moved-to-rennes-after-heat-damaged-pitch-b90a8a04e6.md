- PSG's Ligue 1 home opener against Rennes on Sunday has been moved to Rennes' Roazhon Park.
- The move is due to the heat-damaged pitch at Parc des Princes.
- The change was announced by the LFP on Wednesday.
- PSG expects the return fixture in March to be moved to Paris, but no formal agreement is yet in place.
- PSG's next home game in Ligue 1 is against Monaco on 4 September; the club says it has identified a solution for replacing the pitch.
