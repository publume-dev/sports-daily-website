- The Diamondbacks placed second baseman Ketel Marte on the restricted list on August 17, 2026, after he did not show up for the team's road game against the Boston Red Sox.
- Manager Torey Lovullo said Marte is dealing with a personal issue and has had very little communication with the team.
- In a corresponding move, the Diamondbacks recalled infielder Jose Fernandez from Triple-A, and he went 1 for 3 in the loss.
- The Diamondbacks are 66-60, one game behind the Padres for the third and final NL wild-card spot.
