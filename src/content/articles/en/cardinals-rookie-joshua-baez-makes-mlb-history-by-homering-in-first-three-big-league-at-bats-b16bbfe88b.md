- Joshua Báez became the first player in MLB history to homer in each of his first three big-league at-bats in his debut.
- Báez went 3 for 4 with three home runs and five RBIs in the Cardinals' 8–4 win over the Cubs.
- All three home runs came off Cubs starter Matthew Boyd.
- Báez led the minor leagues with 36 home runs before his promotion.
