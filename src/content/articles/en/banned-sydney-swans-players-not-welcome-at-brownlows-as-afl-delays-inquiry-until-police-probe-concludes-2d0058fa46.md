- The AFL will not invite five Sydney Swans players suspended by their club to this year's Brownlow Medal ceremony.
- The AFL will delay its own inquiry into the players' actions until Victoria Police completes its investigation into an alleged sexual assault.
- The five players—Isaac Heeney, Nick Blakey, Chad Warner, Riley Bice, and James Jordon—were stood down for the rest of the AFL season by the Swans for breaching club standards.

The outcome of the police investigation and any potential AFL sanctions are still pending, and the players remain eligible for the Brownlow award.
