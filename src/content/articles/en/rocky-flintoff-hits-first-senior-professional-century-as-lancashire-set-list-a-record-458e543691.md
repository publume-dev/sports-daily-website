- Flintoff, 18, hit 10 fours and seven sixes in his 76-ball 123.
- The innings came against Somerset at Taunton in the One-Day Cup.
- Keaton Jennings made 156 from 127 balls, the top individual score for Lancashire.
- Lancashire finished on 443-6, setting a new highest List A total for the county.
