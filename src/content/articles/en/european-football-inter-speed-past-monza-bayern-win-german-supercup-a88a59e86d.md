- Inter Milan opened their Serie A title defence with a 4-1 home win against Monza, goals from Hakan Calhanoglu, Piotr Zielinski, Pio Esposito, and Yann Bisseck.
- Napoli won 2-0 at Genoa, with Kevin De Bruyne scoring and assisting Antonio Vergara's goal.
- Bayern Munich beat Borussia Dortmund 2-1 to win the German Supercup, with Michael Olise scoring once and assisting Nathaniel Brown.
- Sevilla won 3-1 at Athletic Bilbao, with Oso scoring one and setting up two after Yuri Berchiche's red card.
- Lens beat Auxerre 5-2 in Ligue 1, with two penalties from Florian Thauvin; Auxerre's Christ Makosso was sent off.
