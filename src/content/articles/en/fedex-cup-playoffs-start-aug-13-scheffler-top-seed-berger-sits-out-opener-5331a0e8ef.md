- The St. Jude Championship runs Aug. 13-16, the BMW Championship Aug. 20-23, and the Tour Championship Aug. 27-30.
- Scheffler enters as the No. 1 seed on 4,123 FedEx Cup points.
- Daniel Berger decided to skip the St. Jude Championship, so 69 of the top 70 eligible players will compete.
- The Tour Championship will be 72 holes of stroke play without a staggered start, with a $40 million purse and $10 million winner's share.
- The St. Jude Championship and BMW Championship each carry a $20 million purse and a $3.6 million winner's share.
- Data Golf projects a 100% chance that players ranked in the top 18 reach the Tour Championship, though that is a projection rather than an official clinch.
