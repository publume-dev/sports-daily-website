- Adam Ramsay-Peaty finished fifth in the 100m breaststroke final at the European Aquatics Championships in Paris, his first-ever loss at a European Championships.
- Nicolo Martinenghi won the 100m breaststroke in 58.58 seconds, with Ivan Kozhakin second and Simone Cerasuolo third.
- Great Britain's Katie Shanahan won gold in the 200m backstroke with a time of 2:06.13, finishing 1.49 seconds ahead of the field.
- Angharad Evans set a championship record of 1:04.97 in the women's 100m breaststroke semi-final.
