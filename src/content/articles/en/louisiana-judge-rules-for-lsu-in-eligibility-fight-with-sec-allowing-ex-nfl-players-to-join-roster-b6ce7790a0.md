- A Louisiana judge granted a preliminary injunction against the SEC, blocking enforcement of its ban on former professional athletes and allowing LSU to add tight end Dae'Quan Wright and defensive lineman Zxavian Harris to its roster.
- The SEC said it will comply with the order while continuing to fight to enforce its rules, and it plans to file an appeal.
- Georgia Attorney General Chris Carr called on SEC member institutions to use all measures available, including suspension or expulsion, if LSU plays former professional athletes.
- Sources told CBS Sports that other SEC schools have pondered a legal challenge in federal court and possible forfeiture of games if LSU plays the former NFL players.
