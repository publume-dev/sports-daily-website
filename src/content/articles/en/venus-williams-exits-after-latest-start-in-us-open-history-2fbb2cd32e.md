- Venus Williams lost to Sofia Kenin 6-2, 7-6(6) in the first round of the 2026 US Open, with the match starting at 12:13am, the latest start in US Open history.
- Williams' tour-level losing streak extended to 15 matches; she has not won a major match since Wimbledon 2021.
- Kenin, the 2020 Australian Open champion, will face No. 3 seed Jessica Pegula in the second round.
- Williams is scheduled to play women's doubles with sister Serena starting Thursday.
