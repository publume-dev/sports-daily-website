- López won by majority decision (115-113, 116-112, 114-114) over Romero to claim the WBA welterweight title.
- López became a three-division world champion, having previously held titles at lightweight and junior welterweight.
- Compubox credited López with 124 punches landed to Romero's 73, including a career-high 62 jabs.
- Romero disputed the result, calling it a robbery, but the scorecards and statistics support the decision.
