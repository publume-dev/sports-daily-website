- The One-Day Cup group stage concluded on 11 August 2026; Lancashire, Yorkshire, Durham and Nottinghamshire took the remaining quarter-final places, joining already-qualified Leicestershire and Middlesex.
- Lancashire posted a record List A score of 443-6 against Somerset at Taunton, their highest ever, with centuries from Keaton Jennings (156) and Rocky Flintoff (123).
- Middlesex beat Sussex by 92 runs at Radlett, finished top of Group B and earned a home semi-final on Sunday — their first List A semi-final since 1990.
- Nottinghamshire chased down 299 to beat Surrey at the Oval, George Munsey scoring 138, to secure qualification from Group A.
- Yorkshire secured second place in Group B by beating Essex by seven wickets and will host a home quarter-final at York on Friday.
- Defending champions Worcestershire were eliminated despite beating Durham by 77 runs; Durham qualified narrowly by passing 204 before being bowled out for 214.
