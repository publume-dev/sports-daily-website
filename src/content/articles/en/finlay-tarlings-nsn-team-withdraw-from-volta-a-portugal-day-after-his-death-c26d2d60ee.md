- Finlay Tarling's NSN Development team withdrew from the remaining stages of the Volta a Portugal on Saturday, the day after his death in an accident during the race.
- The NSN statement said the World Tour team will continue racing at the Czech Tour and the Arctic Race of Norway, with the full support of Fin's parents.
- The race resumed on Saturday with riders wearing black armbands and a minute of silence observed before the penultimate stage.
- The exact circumstances of the accident are still under investigation, with Portuguese authorities stating they do not yet know exactly how it unfolded.
