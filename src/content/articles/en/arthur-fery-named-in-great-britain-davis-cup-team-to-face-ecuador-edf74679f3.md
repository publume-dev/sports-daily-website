- Arthur Fery has been named in Great Britain's Davis Cup team for the tie against Ecuador in September at London's Copper Box Arena.
- The tie takes place over two days on 19 and 20 September, with two singles on day one and a doubles followed by two singles on day two.
- Jack Draper is not included due to a persistent arm injury that has dropped his ranking from fourth to 160th.

A fifth player is expected to be added to the team closer to the time, though no names or timeline have been provided.
