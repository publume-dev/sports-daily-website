- BBC Sport reports that Ulster captain Iain Henderson will miss several months after undergoing hip surgery; no return date beyond that has been given.
- Bryn Ward, Juarno Augustus, and Jude Postlethwaite have also had surgery and are currently in rehabilitation.

- Ulster begin their URC season at home to Edinburgh on 25 September.
