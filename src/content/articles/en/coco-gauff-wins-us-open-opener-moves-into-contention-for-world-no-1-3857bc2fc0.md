- Coco Gauff won her US Open first-round match 6-3, 6-4 against Zeynep Sonmez on Tuesday.
- Gauff is one of four women who can leave the tournament as world No 1, along with Elena Rybakina, Jessica Pegula, and Aryna Sabalenka.
- To secure the top ranking, Gauff must win the title and Rybakina must lose before the semi-finals.
- Gauff's next opponent will be Daria Kasatkina or Paula Badosa.
