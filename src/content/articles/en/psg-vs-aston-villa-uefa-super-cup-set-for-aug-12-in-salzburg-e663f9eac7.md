- CBS Sports reports PSG and Aston Villa face each other in the UEFA Super Cup at Red Bull Arena in Salzburg on Wednesday, Aug. 12 at 3 p.m. ET.
- The match will be streamed on Paramount+ and televised on CBS Sports Network.
- PSG are the 2025-26 Champions League winners; Aston Villa are the Europa League title holders.
- PSG coach Luis Enrique said the team had many pre-season absences and he did not know the physical condition of his players.
