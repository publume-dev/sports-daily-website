- An Indiana judge granted a temporary restraining order restoring eligibility for Indiana edge rushers Stephen Daley and Kellan Wyatt, allowing them to play in the Hoosiers' season opener against North Texas.
- Daley and Wyatt are listed on Indiana's official roster, and both are expected to be available for the season opener on Saturday.
- Indiana's remaining 2026 schedule includes games against five quarterbacks ranked in the top half of CBS Sports' Big Ten quarterback rankings, starting with Ohio State on Oct. 17 and including Michigan, Minnesota, USC, and Washington.

The article does not specify whether the NCAA will appeal this temporary restraining order or if further legal action could affect the players' eligibility during the season.
