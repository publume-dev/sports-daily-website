- Jonny Brownlee has retired from professional triathlon at age 36.
- He won Olympic bronze at London 2012, silver at Rio 2016, and gold in the mixed relay at Tokyo 2020.
- He was a six-time world champion and won mixed team relay gold at the 2014 Commonwealth Games.
- Brownlee cited the birth of his son Freddie 10 months ago and a shift in priorities as factors in his decision.
