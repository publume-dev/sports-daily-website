- The club confirmed Chermiti suffered anterior cruciate ligament damage in Sunday's 2-1 home loss to Hibernian and will be out for a 'significant period'.
- Chermiti, 22, joined Rangers from Everton in September 2025 and has 15 goals in 43 appearances for the club.
- Rangers turned down a reported £22m bid for Chermiti on the day he was injured; manager Derek McInnes said the club's valuation had not been met, with Galatasaray reported as the interested club.
- The club has not disclosed a recovery timeline, only saying the striker will be sidelined for a significant period.
- The reported bidder and the exact valuation have not been officially confirmed.

The injury rules Chermiti out of Rangers' Europa League qualifier against Jagiellonia Bialystok.
