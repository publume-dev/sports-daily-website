- The SEC will contest the Louisiana court order and defend its rules against former professional players at a Thursday morning hearing.
- A Louisiana judge issued a temporary restraining order barring the SEC from punishing schools that play former NFL players, with a show-cause hearing scheduled for Thursday.
- The SEC's rule change, passed 15-0 by presidents, would suspend coaches for half a season and fine schools up to 50% of a sport's operating budget for playing pros.
- LSU could roster up to three former NFL players if the court order holds, including Jack Pyburn and Dae'Quan Wright.
