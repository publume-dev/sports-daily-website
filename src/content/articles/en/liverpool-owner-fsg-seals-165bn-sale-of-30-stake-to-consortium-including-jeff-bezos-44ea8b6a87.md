- Fenway Sports Group has agreed to sell a 30% stake in Liverpool to a consortium led by Amit Bhatia, including Jeff Bezos and Eduardo Saverin, for £1.65bn, valuing the club at £5.5bn.
- The deal is subject to regulatory approval, which could take up to 90 days, and will not affect Liverpool's transfer budget or strategy for this summer.
- Amit Bhatia will become vice-chair of Liverpool, and Elaine Saverin and Bryan Baum will join the board; Bezos will be a passive investor without a board seat.
- FSG will retain operational control and majority ownership of Liverpool, and the consortium will have options to purchase more shares only if FSG decides to sell in the future.
