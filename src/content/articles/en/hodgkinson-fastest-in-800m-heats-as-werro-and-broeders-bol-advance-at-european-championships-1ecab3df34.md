- Keely Hodgkinson won her women's 800m heat at the European Athletics Championships in Birmingham in 1:57.28, the fastest time across the four heats on Tuesday morning.
- Audrey Werro won her heat in 1:57.83 and Femke Broeders-Bol won hers in 1:59.96, with all three advancing to Thursday's semi-finals.
- Hodgkinson, seeking a fifth consecutive European 800m title, has a day off before Thursday's semi-final.

- The women's 800m final is scheduled for Friday night.
