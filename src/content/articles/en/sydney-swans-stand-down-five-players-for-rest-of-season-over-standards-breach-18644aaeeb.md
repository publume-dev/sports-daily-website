- The Sydney Swans have stood down five players — Riley Bice, Nick Blakey, Isaac Heeney, James Jordon, and Chad Warner — for the remainder of the AFL season, effective Wednesday.
- The players are linked to a Victoria Police investigation into an alleged sexual assault at the Pullman Hotel in Melbourne, but have not been arrested or charged.
- Swans CEO Matthew Pavlich said the club is 'deeply embarrassed and ashamed' and that the players breached club standards by staying out late, drinking, and bringing women back to the hotel.
- The club issued a statement saying the actions are independent of the ongoing police investigation, with the AFL supportive of the sanctions.

The Swans secured a home final with a win over Essendon on Sunday, and the suspensions will affect their finals campaign.
