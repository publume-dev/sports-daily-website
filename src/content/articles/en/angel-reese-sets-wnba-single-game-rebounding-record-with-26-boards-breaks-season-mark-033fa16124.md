- On August 24, Angel Reese recorded 26 rebounds in the Atlanta Dream's 78-71 victory over the Los Angeles Sparks, setting a WNBA single-game rebounding record.
- Reese surpassed A'ja Wilson's 2024 mark for the most rebounds in a single season, now totaling 458 with six games remaining.
- The win completed a 4-0 West Coast road trip for the Dream, improving their record to 25-13 and moving them into sole possession of fourth place in the playoff standings.
