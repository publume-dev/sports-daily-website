- Scottie Scheffler shot a nine-under-par 61 in the second round of the St Jude Championship, equalling the course record, to lead at 11 under par, three strokes ahead of the field.
- The top 50 players on the FedEx Cup points list after the St Jude Championship will advance to next week's BMW Championship.
- Robert MacIntyre withdrew from the St Jude Championship before the second round with a knee injury, after shooting a 12-over 82 in the first round.
