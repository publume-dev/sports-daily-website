- Great Britain's Angharad Evans won gold in the women's 100m breaststroke at the European Aquatics Championships in Paris, setting a championship record, lifetime best, and British record of 1:04.87.
- Ireland's Mona McSharry took silver and Italy's Benedetta Pilato bronze in that event.
- Italy's Sara Curtis broke the women's 50m backstroke world record with 26.63 seconds in her semi-final, bettering the previous record by 0.23 seconds.
- Ireland's Ellen Walshe won gold in the women's 400m individual medley, beating Great Britain's Amalie Smith, who took silver.
- Great Britain's Filip Nowacki was the fastest qualifier for the men's 200m breaststroke final with a time of 2:08.60.
