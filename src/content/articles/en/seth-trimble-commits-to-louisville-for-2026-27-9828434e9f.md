- Former North Carolina guard Seth Trimble committed to Louisville on Aug. 12, 2026, for the 2026-27 season.
- Trimble averaged 14 points, 3.8 rebounds, 3 assists and 1.3 steals per game as a senior at North Carolina.
- A federal judge's preliminary injunction requires the NCAA to grant athletes from the 2022 recruiting cycle an additional year of eligibility.
- Louisville's 2026-27 roster features the No. 1 transfer class per 247Sports, headlined by Kansas transfer Flory Bidunga.
- Trimble signed an Exhibit 10 deal with the Washington Wizards and joined their Las Vegas Summer League roster.
