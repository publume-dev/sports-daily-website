- Mexico retained the Concacaf Under-20 Championship with a 2-0 win over the United States in the final at the Azteca.
- Cristóbal Alfaro scored both goals, with headers in the 28th and 69th minutes.
- The victory booked Mexico's place at the 2028 Los Angeles Olympics; the US had already qualified as hosts.
- US captain Chris Applewhite was shown a red card after a VAR review for a challenge on Hugo Camberos just before half-time.
- Mexico finished the tournament unbeaten, conceding only one goal.
- Both teams had already secured places in the 2027 Under-20 World Cup in Azerbaijan and Uzbekistan by reaching the semi-finals.
