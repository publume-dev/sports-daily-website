- Victoria Police confirmed an ongoing investigation into an alleged sexual assault in East Melbourne on 17 August involving Sydney Swans players; no arrests or charges have been made.
- Sydney Swans coach Dean Cox expressed extreme disappointment and said the club is working with the AFL and relevant parties on possible sanctions.
- Players Isaac Heeney, Chad Warner, James Jordon, and Riley Bice were reportedly questioned by detectives, but their direct involvement is unconfirmed.
- The Swans play North Melbourne at the MCG on Sunday, with squad naming due Thursday and Friday, followed by a week off before a home qualifying final.

If players are confirmed involved and face sanctions, it could affect the Swans' upcoming finals campaign, as key players may be suspended.

Watch for whether the Swans announce sanctions on Thursday regarding the involved players, and for updates from the police investigation.
