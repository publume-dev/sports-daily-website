- The Dodgers are placing closer Edwin Díaz on the injured list with neck inflammation, manager Dave Roberts told reporters on Tuesday.
- Díaz has an 11.85 ERA, 2.56 WHIP and -1.4 WAR in 16 outings this season after signing a three-year, $69 million contract.
- Díaz will return to Los Angeles to be evaluated on Wednesday.
- In a corresponding move, Emmet Sheehan will be recalled from Triple-A to provide help for the Dodgers' bullpen.
