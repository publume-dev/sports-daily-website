- The Los Angeles Lakers are reportedly being sold for $12.5 billion to Josh Kushner and Bob Iger, though the exact price is not officially confirmed by the team or league.
- The deal surpasses the previous record of $10 billion paid by Mark Walter in 2025 for the Lakers.
- The sale requires approval from the NBA Board of Governors, whose next meeting is in September.
