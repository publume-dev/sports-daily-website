- Trainer George Boughey announced on 11 August 2026 that Bow Echo has been retired from racing after a training setback.
- Bow Echo finishes his career unbeaten in six starts, with wins in the 2000 Guineas, St James’s Palace Stakes and Sussex Stakes.

Bow Echo will join Dalham Hall Stud for Darley, Godolphin’s breeding arm.
