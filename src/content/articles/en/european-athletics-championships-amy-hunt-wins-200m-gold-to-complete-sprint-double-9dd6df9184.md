- Amy Hunt of Great Britain won the women's 200m final in a season's best 22.19 seconds. 
- Ireland's Rhasidat Adeleke took silver in 22.28 seconds, a new national record; Dina Asher-Smith of Great Britain took bronze in 22.29 seconds. 
- Hunt's victory adds to her 100m gold from Monday, and she aims to become the first athlete to win four European gold medals at the same championships. 
- Asher-Smith's bronze is her ninth European medal, a tally only bettered by Polish sprinter Irena Szewinska's ten.
