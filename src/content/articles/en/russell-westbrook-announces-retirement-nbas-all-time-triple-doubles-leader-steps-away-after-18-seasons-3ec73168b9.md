- Westbrook announced his retirement on August 12, 2026, via a social media video, ending an 18-season NBA career.
- He finishes as the NBA's all-time leader in triple-doubles with 209.
- His career totals include 27,176 points (14th all-time) and 10,351 assists (5th all-time).
- He is one of only three players to average a triple-double for a full season, and he did it four times, more than anyone else.
- He was a nine-time All-Star, the 2017 NBA MVP, and won two scoring titles and two All-Star Game MVPs.
