- Sir Billy Boston, the first rugby league player to be knighted, has died at the age of 92.
- He scored 478 tries in 488 appearances for Wigan, and 571 tries in his career, a total surpassed only by Brian Bevan.
- He won the Rugby League World Cup with Great Britain in 1960, scoring a try in the final against Australia.
- Wigan plan tributes at their Super League game against Hull KR on Thursday evening.
