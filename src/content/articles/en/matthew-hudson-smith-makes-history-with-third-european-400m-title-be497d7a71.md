- Matthew Hudson-Smith won the European Championships 400m gold in 44.17 seconds in Birmingham.
- He became the first man to win three European Championship 400m titles.
- With his eighth European Championships podium, he equalled Christophe Lemaitre's men's record medal tally.
- Muhammad Abdallah Kounta of France took silver in 44.40 seconds.
- Emily Newnham won silver in the women's 400m hurdles with a personal best of 53.33 seconds.
