- Root has abolished the midnight curfew for the Test team, stating "There's not going to be a curfew."
- The curfew was initially imposed after England's Ashes defeat, following incidents including Harry Brook's altercation with a nightclub bouncer and suspensions for Ben Stokes and Gus Atkinson.
- Root argued curfews "don't work" and emphasized that players should "be adults" and self-police, with the relaxation also applying to white-ball sides.

The England and Wales Cricket Board has not yet officially confirmed the lifting of the curfew, and the exact timing remains unspecified.
