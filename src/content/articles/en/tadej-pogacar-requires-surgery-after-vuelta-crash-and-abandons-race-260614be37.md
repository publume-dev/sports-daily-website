- Tadej Pogacar requires surgery on a broken collarbone after crashing on stage eight of the Vuelta a Espana, also suffering a concussion and a stable neck fracture.
- Pogacar abandoned the Vuelta, the first time he has abandoned a Grand Tour.
- Surgery on Pogacar's clavicle will be delayed due to concussion precautions.
- Enric Mas now leads the general classification by 1 minute 11 seconds from Felix Gall.
- Bryan Coquard won stage eight of the Vuelta a Espana, his first Grand Tour stage win.
