- The Tampa Bay Rays reached a $2.3 billion deal for a new stadium and surrounding development in Tampa, with an ideal opening for the 2029 season.
- Hillsborough County commissioners voted 5-2 to approve county funding, and the Tampa City Council voted 4-3 to approve city funding.
- The city of Tampa will contribute roughly $80 million and Hillsborough County nearly $800 million, with the remaining $1.4 billion privately financed.
- The new ballpark will replace Tropicana Field and will be located near Hillsborough College's Dale Mabry campus.
