- Ronnie O'Sullivan lost 6-4 to Noppon Saengkham in the China Open second round, having led 3-1.
- World champion Wu Yize was eliminated after a 6-2 loss to David Gilbert.
- Noppon Saengkham, ranked 45th, will face Shaun Murphy in the next round after Murphy defeated Xiao Guodong 6-2.
