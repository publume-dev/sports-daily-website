- The Rays lead the AL East, the White Sox lead the AL Central, and the Astros lead the AL West, with each division race within four games.
- In the National League, the Brewers lead the NL Central, the Braves lead the NL East by four games over the Phillies, and the Dodgers have clinched the NL West.
- The Toronto Blue Jays currently hold the final AL wild-card spot, tied with the Guardians at 72-71 but owning the head-to-head tiebreaker.
- The current playoff bracket would feature AL byes for the Rays and White Sox, and NL byes for the Brewers and Braves, with wild-card series matchups including the Astros vs. Blue Jays and Yankees vs. Red Sox in the AL, and Dodgers vs. Padres and Cubs vs. Phillies in the NL.

The rankings and bracket could shift with remaining regular-season results, especially in the tight wild-card races.
