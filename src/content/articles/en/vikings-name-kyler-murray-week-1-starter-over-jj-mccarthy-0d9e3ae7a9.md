- According to CBS Sports, head coach Kevin O'Connell named Kyler Murray the Week 1 starting quarterback over J.J. McCarthy on Tuesday, Aug. 11, 2026.
- O'Connell made the call before Minnesota's preseason opener against the New York Giants on Saturday, without waiting for either quarterback to play in that game.
- McCarthy ranked last in the NFL in 2025 in completion percentage (57.6%), touchdown-to-interception ratio (11-12) and passer rating (72.6).
- Murray missed 30 of 68 possible games from 2022 to 2025, including the final 12 games of 2025 with a foot injury.
