- The Washington Mystics have won seven consecutive games, tied for the second-longest winning streak in franchise history, and moved to fifth place in the WNBA standings.
- The Dallas Wings have lost three straight games and six of their last eight, dropping to eighth place.
- Kayla McBride made a WNBA single-game record 10 three-pointers and scored a career-high 43 points in Minnesota's win over Dallas.
- Kelsey Mitchell extended the WNBA record for consecutive 20-point games to 16, surpassing A'ja Wilson.
- The Seattle Storm became the first team eliminated from playoff contention after an 11-game losing streak.
