- Clark scored 37 points and made a career-high eight 3-pointers in the Fever's 113-90 win over the Sky.
- Clark recorded her fifth career 30-point, 10-assist game, the most in WNBA history.
- The Fever made 18 three-pointers, a new single-game franchise record.
- With the win, the Fever improved to 25-14, setting a new single-season wins record for the franchise.
