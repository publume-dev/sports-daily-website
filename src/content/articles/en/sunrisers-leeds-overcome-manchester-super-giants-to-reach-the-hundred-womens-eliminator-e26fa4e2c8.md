- Sunrisers Leeds beat Manchester Super Giants by 41 runs at Old Trafford.
- Annabel Sutherland made an unbeaten 62 off 36 balls for Sunrisers Leeds.
- Jess Jonassen took 3-21 for Sunrisers Leeds, including the wickets of Smriti Mandhana and Richa Ghosh.

- Sunrisers Leeds will face Southern Brave in the Eliminator at The Oval on Friday; the winner will play Trent Rockets in Sunday's final at Lord's.
