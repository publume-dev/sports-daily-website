- Rodri has moved from Manchester City to Barcelona on a four-year deal running until 30 June 2030.
- Barcelona will pay a reported fee of around £65m, structured as €60m plus €15m in add-ons.
- The 2024 Ballon d'Or winner was named the best player at the 2026 World Cup as Spain triumphed.
- Rodri leaves Manchester City after seven seasons, having made 298 appearances and won 12 major honours, including four Premier League titles and the Champions League.
