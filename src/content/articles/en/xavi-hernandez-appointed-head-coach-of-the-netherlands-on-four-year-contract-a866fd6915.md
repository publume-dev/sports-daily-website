- Xavi Hernández has been appointed head coach of the Netherlands on a four-year contract through to the 2030 World Cup.
- He replaces Ronald Koeman, who stepped down following the Netherlands' last-32 defeat by Morocco at the 2026 World Cup.
- Xavi, a World Cup winner as a player with Spain in 2010, previously coached Barcelona to the La Liga title in 2023.
- Former Liverpool head coach Arne Slot walked away from talks about replacing Koeman, saying he prefers club football.
