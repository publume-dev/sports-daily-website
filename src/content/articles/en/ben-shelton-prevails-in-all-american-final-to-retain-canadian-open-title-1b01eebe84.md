- Ben Shelton beat Brandon Nakashima 6-3, 7-6 (4) to win the Canadian Open, successfully defending his title and earning his second Masters 1000 crown.
- Shelton is the fourth male player this century to win the Canadian title back-to-back, joining Rafael Nadal (2018-19), Andy Murray (2009-10), and Novak Djokovic (2011-12).
- Shelton won his fourth tournament of the season (Dallas, Munich, Stuttgart, and now Montreal), recording 22 winners and 12 unforced errors in the final.
- In the first round of the Cincinnati Open, Cameron Norrie defeated Dino Prizmic 3-6, 6-1, 6-4 and will face top-seeded Alexander Zverev next.
- Martin Landaluce ended a six-match losing streak by defeating Jack Draper 6-3, 7-5 in the first round of the Cincinnati Open.
