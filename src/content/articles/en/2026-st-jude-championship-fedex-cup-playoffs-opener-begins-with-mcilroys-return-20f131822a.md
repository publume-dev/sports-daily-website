- The 2026 St. Jude Championship is the first event of the FedEx Cup Playoffs, held in Memphis with 69 players competing; the top 50 advance to the BMW Championship.
- Rory McIlroy, who skipped the event in 2025, has committed to play and begins the week 12th in the FedEx Cup standings.
- Daniel Berger is the only notable absence, having been 60th in the standings but opting not to commit to the Memphis field.
- The cut line battle includes Michael Brennan, Matt McCarty, Keith Mitchell, and Maverick McNealy occupying the last four spots, with Harris English, Max Homa, Sungjae Im, and Jordan Spieth just outside.
- TV coverage: early ESPN coverage Thursday and Friday, live Golf Channel in the afternoon, and CBS/Paramount+ carrying live TV from 3-6 p.m. ET on Saturday and Sunday.
