- Crow-Armstrong hit three home runs and drove in six runs in the Cubs' 15-5 win over the Reds, becoming the first Cubs player since 1974 to record at least four hits, three homers, and six RBI in a game at Wrigley Field.
- Entering the game, Crow-Armstrong led the majors with a 7.9 WAR and was batting .282/.377/.567 with 36 home runs and 32 stolen bases in 136 games.
- He is the fourth player in the last 50 years to record at least 12 homers, 25 RBI, and six steals in a single calendar month, joining Ryne Sandberg (1990), Eric Davis (1987), and Bobby Bonds (1977).
- Following the three-homer game, his odds to win NL MVP shortened to -1100, while Shohei Ohtani is at +650.
