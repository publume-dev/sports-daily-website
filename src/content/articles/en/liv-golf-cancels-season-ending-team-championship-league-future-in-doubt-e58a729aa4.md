- LIV Golf has canceled its $40 million season-ending team championship scheduled for Aug. 27-30 at The Cardinal at St. John's, marking the second tournament cancellation in 2026.
- The Indianapolis tournament on Aug. 20-23 at The Club at Chatham Hills will now be the final event of the 2026 season.
- LIV Golf told players about the cancellation according to The Telegraph, and several players are reportedly unaware if the $40 million in team prizes will be paid out.
- LIV Golf CEO Scott O'Neil stated that a lead investor has signed an agreement to anchor a transaction for the league's future, with more than a dozen additional parties interested as minority investors, and players becoming majority equity holders.
- Saudi Arabia's Public Investment Fund will stop funding LIV Golf at the end of the 2026 season, and the league is reportedly seeking $250 million to $300 million in outside investment.
