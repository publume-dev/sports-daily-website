- The NBA released portions of its 2026-27 regular-season schedule: Opening Night on Oct. 20 and Christmas Day games.
- Opening Night is a tripleheader: Celtics at Pistons, 76ers at Knicks, Thunder at Spurs.
- The defending champion Knicks open at home against the 76ers.
- Christmas Day has five games: Spurs at Knicks, 76ers at Lakers, Heat at Celtics, Thunder at Timberwolves, Nuggets at Warriors.
- The NBA Cup championship game moves from Las Vegas to Hinkle Fieldhouse in Indianapolis.

- The full regular-season schedule is expected to be released Thursday.
