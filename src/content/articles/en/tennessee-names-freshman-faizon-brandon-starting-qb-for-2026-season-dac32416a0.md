- Tennessee has named freshman Faizon Brandon as its starting quarterback for the 2026 season, beating out sophomore George MacIntyre.
- Brandon, a five-star recruit and No. 2 quarterback in the 2026 class, posted a 35-1 record as a high school starter with 90 touchdown passes and 10 interceptions.
- Brandon will make his first start against Furman on Sept. 5, followed by a road game at Georgia Tech on Sept. 12 and the SEC opener vs. No. 5 Texas on Sept. 26.
