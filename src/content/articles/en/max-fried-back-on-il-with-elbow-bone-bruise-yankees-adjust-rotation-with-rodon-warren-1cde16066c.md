- Max Fried was placed on the injured list due to a bone bruise in his pitching elbow, an injury that sidelined him for two months earlier this season.
- Manager Aaron Boone said the injury is "not like that time" and Fried could miss only the requisite 15 days.
- Carlos Rodón is scheduled to return from the injured list Tuesday and will step into Fried's rotation spot.
- Will Warren will remain in the rotation rather than moving to the bullpen as previously expected.
- Since the All-Star break, the Yankees have allowed an MLB-best 3.39 runs per game, but have scored three or fewer runs in 19 of 28 games.
