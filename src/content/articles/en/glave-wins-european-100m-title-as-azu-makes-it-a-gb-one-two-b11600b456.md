- Romell Glave won the 2026 European Athletics Championships men's 100m gold in 10.09 seconds.
- Jeremiah Azu finished second in 10.16, completing a Great Britain one-two.
- Germany's Owen Ansah took bronze after Italy's Lamont Marcell Jacobs pulled up injured.
- Glave became the sixth British man to win the European 100m title.
- Nadia Battocletti retained the women's 5,000m title; Britain's Hannah Nuttall finished fifth.
- Matthew Hudson-Smith qualified for Wednesday's men's 400m final by finishing second in his semi-final.
- Nadine Visser won the women's 100m hurdles by 0.001 seconds from Pia Skrzyszowska.
