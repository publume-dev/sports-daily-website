- Nick Kyrgios tested positive for benzoylecgonine, a cocaine metabolite, at June's Mallorca grass-court event.
- He is under provisional suspension starting 4 August.
- If he proves he took cocaine out of competition, the ban could be as short as one month with a treatment programme, potentially making him eligible from 4 September.

- The report is based on a single source and does not confirm whether Kyrgios will provide evidence of out-of-competition use.
