- Terrion Arnold plans to sign a one-year deal with the Seattle Seahawks, per CBS Sports senior reporter Matt Zenitz.
- Arnold was released by the Lions after being arrested on felony charges tied to a February robbery and kidnapping; he faces a possible suspension from NFL commissioner Roger Goodell.
- Arnold has an arraignment hearing set for Thursday in Tampa and remains subject to a potential suspension.
- Arnold recorded one interception and 18 pass breakups in 24 games (22 starts) with the Lions, and played eight games last season before a season-ending shoulder injury.
- In Seattle, Arnold would join a secondary that ranked fifth in interceptions, seventh in touchdown passes allowed, and 10th in passing yards allowed last season.
