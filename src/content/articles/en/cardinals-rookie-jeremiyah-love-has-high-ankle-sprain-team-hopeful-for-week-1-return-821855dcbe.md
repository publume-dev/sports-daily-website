- Cardinals rookie running back Jeremiyah Love suffered a high ankle sprain in Thursday's preseason game and is unlikely to play again this preseason.
- Coach Mike LaFleur said Love will not practice this week and will miss the Week 2 preseason game against the Cowboys, but did not rule him out for Week 1 against the Chargers on Sept. 13.

Love's availability for Week 1 is uncertain; coach LaFleur did not commit to him playing.
