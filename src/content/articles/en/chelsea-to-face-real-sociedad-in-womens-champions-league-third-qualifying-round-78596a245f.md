- The Guardian reports that Chelsea's tie with Real Sociedad will begin at home on 26 August and finish in Spain on 2 September.
- The winner over two legs will earn a place in the Women's Champions League league phase.
- Chelsea are in qualifying after finishing third in the Women's Super League last season, their lowest league finish since 2019.
- All 18 league-phase clubs are guaranteed €505,000, plus €60,000 per win and €20,000 per draw.

- Next: Chelsea open their Women's Super League season at home against Aston Villa on 5 September.
