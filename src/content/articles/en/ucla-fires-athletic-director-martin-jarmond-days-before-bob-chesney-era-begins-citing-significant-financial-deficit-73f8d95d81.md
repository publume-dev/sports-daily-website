- UCLA has fired athletic director Martin Jarmond, citing a significant financial deficit and unsustainable path.
- Former UCLA soccer player and Los Angeles Lakers executive Tim Harris will replace Jarmond on a pro bono basis.
- Jarmond is owed the remaining $6.5 million on his contract, according to ESPN.
- The move comes days before new football coach Bob Chesney makes his UCLA debut against Cal on Saturday night in Berkeley.
