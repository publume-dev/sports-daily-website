- A competitor died following a crash involving two riders during qualifying for the Manx Grand Prix on 18 August 2026.
- The crash occurred at Cruickshanks, Ramsey, and the session was red-flagged at about 18:50 BST.
- The other rider suffered minor injuries and was treated at Noble's Hospital; the riders have not been named.
- A multi-agency investigative process is underway, per event organisers.
- Qualifying is scheduled daily until Saturday, with racing in the Manx Grand Prix and Classic TT until 28 August.
