- Jack Whaley of England won the 2026 U.S. Amateur at Merion, beating Jay Leng Jr. 4 and 3 in the 36-hole final.
- Whaley, a 22-year-old Florida State senior, earned exemptions into the 2027 Masters, U.S. Open, and Open Championship at St Andrews, provided he remains an amateur.
- He is the first English winner of the U.S. Amateur since Matt Fitzpatrick in 2013.

The victory gives Whaley a spot in three major championships in 2027.
