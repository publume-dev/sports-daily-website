- Mark Mitchell, a former Duke and Missouri forward, has committed to play for Kentucky in the 2026-27 season.
- Mitchell averaged 18.3 points, 5.2 rebounds, and 3.6 assists per game last season in the SEC.
- Mitchell received a temporary restraining order from a Jefferson Circuit Court in Kentucky on Friday to pursue a fifth season, and his eligibility remains subject to ongoing legal review.

Mitchell's eligibility for the upcoming season is not fully guaranteed; the NCAA may still challenge the court ruling, so his participation depends on the legal outcome.
