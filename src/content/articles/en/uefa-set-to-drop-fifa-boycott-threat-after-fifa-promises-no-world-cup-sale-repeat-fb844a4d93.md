- UEFA is expected to drop its threat of boycotting FIFA competitions after receiving assurances that FIFA will not repeat the plan to sell stakes in the World Cup to private investors.
- The matter will be discussed at a meeting of UEFA's executive committee in Monaco on Thursday.
- The decision could clear the way for England, France, Italy, Portugal, Spain, and hosts Poland to participate in the upcoming U20 Women's World Cup in Poland.
- England's scheduled opening game against Canada in the U20 Women's World Cup is set for 5 September.

If approved by UEFA's executive committee, European teams will be able to participate in the U20 Women's World Cup, avoiding absence due to the boycott.

Watch for the outcome of Thursday's UEFA executive committee meeting, which will formally decide whether to lift the boycott.
