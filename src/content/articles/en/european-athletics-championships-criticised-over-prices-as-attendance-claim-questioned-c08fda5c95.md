- The Guardian reports that more than 13,000 fans attended Monday evening's session, where Amy Hunt won 100m gold; however, the official figure is not reconciled with observers' impressions that the 23,000-capacity Alexander Stadium looked barely half-full.
- At the start of the competition, category A tickets in the main stand cost £110, category B £90, category C £60 and category D £40, with top prices rising to £150 for the final three days.
- Birmingham 2026 said 50% of tickets were priced at £30 or lower, adult tickets started at £10, and Friday, Saturday and Sunday evening sessions were approaching sellout.
- Food at Alexander Stadium included an £18.50 barbecue pork roll, a £15 bratwurst hot dog and £3.95 bottled water.
- The park-and-ride scheme cost £22.50, while a £17.50 park-and-walk option left fans a 45-minute walk from the stadium.
