- The 2027 PGA Tour season will open with The American Express from Jan. 21-24, replacing the canceled Sony Open, which moves to PGA Tour Champions.
- The Cadillac Championship will be played March 4-7 at Trump National Doral, swapping dates with the Valspar Championship.
- The 2027 U.S. Open will be held June 17-20 at Pebble Beach Golf Links, and the Open Championship will be played July 15-18 at St Andrews Old Course.
