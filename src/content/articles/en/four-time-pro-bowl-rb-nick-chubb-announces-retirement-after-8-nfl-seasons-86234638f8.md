- Nick Chubb announced his retirement from professional football after eight NFL seasons.
- He was a four-time Pro Bowler and recorded four consecutive 1,000-yard rushing seasons from 2019 to 2022.
- In his best season (2022), Chubb rushed for a career-high 1,525 yards and 12 touchdowns, earning second-team All-Pro honors.
- His career was hampered by significant injuries, including a 2023 knee injury and a 2024 foot fracture.
- Chubb and Jim Brown are the only players in NFL history with 6,000-plus rushing yards and 5-plus yards per carry in their first five seasons.
