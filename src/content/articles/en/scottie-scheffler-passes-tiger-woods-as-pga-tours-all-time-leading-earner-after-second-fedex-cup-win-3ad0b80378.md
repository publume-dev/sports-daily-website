- Scottie Scheffler surpassed Tiger Woods as the PGA Tour's all-time leading money winner after capturing his second FedEx Cup title.
- Scheffler's career earnings are now $130,390,661, more than $11 million ahead of active player Rory McIlroy.
- Scheffler earned $10 million for the FedEx Cup victory and $14.1 million over the last three weeks.
