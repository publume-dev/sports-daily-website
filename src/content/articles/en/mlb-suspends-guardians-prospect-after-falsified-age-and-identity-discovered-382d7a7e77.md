- Major League Baseball has suspended a low-level Cleveland Guardians prospect after uncovering evidence of falsified age and identity, per ESPN.
- The Guardians signed the prospect, then known as David Severino, in December 2025 as a teenager out of the Dominican Republic, believing he was 13 or 14 years old, with a $2.8 million deal not to be completed until 2029.
- The Guardians terminated the agreement in May of this year after learning the prospect was two years older than claimed, and his real name is Daury Severino Damaso.
- The MLB investigation uncovered extreme efforts by the player's handler to make him appear younger, including faking a birth certificate, falsifying medical and school records, filing a fraudulent death certificate, and creating a gravesite scene.
- The scam was allegedly orchestrated by Francisco Nieves, Severino's former trainer, with the approval of the player's parents, per ESPN.
