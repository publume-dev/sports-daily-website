- Per CBS Sports, the Cowboys and defensive tackle Quinnen Williams agreed to a three-year, $105.9 million extension.
- The deal includes $101 million in total guarantees; $63.35 million is fully guaranteed at signing, the most for a defensive tackle at signing in NFL history.
- Williams' $35.3 million average annual value ranks second among interior defensive linemen, behind Jalen Carter's $38 million.
