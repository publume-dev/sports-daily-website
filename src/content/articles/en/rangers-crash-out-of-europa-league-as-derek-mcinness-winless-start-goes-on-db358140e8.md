- Rangers exited the Europa League qualifiers after drawing 1-1 at Ibrox in the second leg, losing 3-2 on aggregate to Jagiellonia Bialystok.
- Derek McInnes has not won in four competitive matches since moving from Hearts in the summer.
- Rangers will face Jablonec of Czechia in the Conference League playoff round.
- Hearts drew 1-1 with Benfica in the second leg but were eliminated 7-2 on aggregate.
- Hearts will meet Rapid Vienna of Austria in the Conference League playoffs.
- Hibernian drew 0-0 with Shkendija in the second leg but advanced 2-1 on aggregate and will face Gent in the Conference League playoff round.
