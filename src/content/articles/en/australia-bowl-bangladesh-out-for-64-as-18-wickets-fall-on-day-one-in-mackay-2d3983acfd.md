- Australia bowled Bangladesh out for 64 on day one in Mackay, with Mitchell Starc taking 6-12.
- Australia closed on 165-8, a lead of 101, with Cameron Green unbeaten on 51.
- Starc moved to 10th on the all-time Test wicket-takers list with 441, surpassing Dale Steyn.
- Marnus Labuschagne was bowled for four, extending his run without a Test century to 43 innings.
- Shoriful Islam took 6-35 for Bangladesh in Australia's first innings.
