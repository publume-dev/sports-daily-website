- Manchester City completed the £125m signing of Enzo Fernández from Chelsea, equalling the British transfer record set by Alexander Isak's move to Liverpool.
- Fernández signed a five-year deal with City and said he is ready to help the club continue winning trophies.
- Chelsea had agreed a £47m fee with Monaco for Lamine Camara, but Monaco reneged on the deal, which Chelsea sources called 'deeply unprofessional'.

The signing is expected to boost Manchester City's Premier League title ambitions while weakening Chelsea's midfield options.
