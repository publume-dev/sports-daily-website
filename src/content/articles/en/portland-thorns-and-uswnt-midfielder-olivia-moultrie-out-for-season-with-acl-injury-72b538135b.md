- Portland Thorns midfielder Olivia Moultrie was placed on the season-ending injury list because of an ACL injury.
- Moultrie injured her right knee during training last week, the team said.
- She had five goals and five assists for the Thorns this season.
- She has made 18 appearances for the United States, scoring five goals.

Watch: Moultrie will likely miss the Concacaf W Championship, which serves as qualifying for next summer's Women's World Cup.
