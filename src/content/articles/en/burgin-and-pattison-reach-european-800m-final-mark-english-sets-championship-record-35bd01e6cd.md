- Great Britain's Max Burgin and Ben Pattison finished first and second in their semifinals to qualify for the European Athletics Championship 800m final.
- Ireland's Mark English also qualified for that final after setting a European Athletics Championship record time for the event.

The 800m final at the European Athletics Championship is next for Burgin, Pattison and English.
