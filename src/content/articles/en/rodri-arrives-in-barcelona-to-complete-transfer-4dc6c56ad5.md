- Rodri has arrived in Barcelona to complete his move from Manchester City, with Barcelona agreeing to pay about £65m (or £65.4m) for the 30-year-old.
- Rodri is expected to sign a four-year contract with Barcelona.
- Rodri said it is his 'dream' to play for Barcelona.
- City accepted Barcelona's third bid after rejecting earlier offers of £38.5m and around £51m.
- The exact transfer fee differs slightly between sources: £65.4m in The Guardian, about £65m in BBC.
