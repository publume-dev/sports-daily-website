- Teófimo López will challenge Rolly Romero for the WBA welterweight title on Saturday night at T-Mobile Arena in Las Vegas.
- López, 29, is moving up to 147lb to seek a title in a third weight division; his record is 22-2 with 13 KOs.
- Romero won the WBA welterweight title in May 2025 with a unanimous decision over Ryan Garcia; his record is 17-2 with 13 KOs, and this will be his first title defense.
- López is coming off a unanimous decision loss to Shakur Stevenson in January 2026, which cost him the WBO and lineal 140lb titles.
- The fight will be broadcast globally on Dazn pay-per-view, and Romero is a roughly 2-1 underdog.
