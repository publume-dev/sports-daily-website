- The potential overhaul includes double-elimination, reseeding, and 20 teams, debuting in 2028 with a fall-to-spring schedule.
- The new format would combine all teams into a single table at season's end, with the top 20 advancing, including a play-in round and a double-elimination round for the top eight.
- The playoffs would start in late April 2028, with MLS Cup scheduled for Sunday, May 21, 2028.
