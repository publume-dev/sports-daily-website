- Fenway Sports Group is close to completing a £1.35bn sale of a 30% stake in Liverpool to a consortium including Jeff Bezos, according to The Guardian.
- The consortium is led by Amit Bhatia and includes Facebook co-founder Eduardo Saverin, the paper reports.
- The Guardian says the deal is effectively agreed and could take up to a month to complete.
- It would be Bezos's first football investment and give him equity in Liverpool.
- No official confirmation has come from Fenway Sports Group or Liverpool; The Guardian says FSG was approached for comment.
