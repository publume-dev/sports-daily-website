- The Maracanã will host the final on July 25, 2027, becoming the third stadium to host both men's and women's World Cup finals.
- Brazil opens the tournament at the Maracanã on June 24 against a yet-to-be-determined opponent, with group stage matches in São Paulo and Brasília.
- The tournament runs June 24–July 25: group stage June 24–July 8, round of 16 July 10–13, quarterfinals July 16–17, semifinals July 20–21, and bronze final July 24.
- FIFA finalized the intercontinental playoff preliminary phase, featuring Uzbekistan, Papua New Guinea, Chinese Taipei, South Africa, Ghana, and Ecuador, with the top two advancing to the final phase in February 2027.
- Eighteen of 32 qualified teams are confirmed, including hosts Brazil, reigning champions Spain, and newcomers Malawi, with remaining spots decided in the December draw.
