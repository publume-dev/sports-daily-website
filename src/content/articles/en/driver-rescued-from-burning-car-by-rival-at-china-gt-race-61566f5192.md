- Ollie Millroy was rescued from a burning Ferrari by rival driver Loek Hartog after a crash in the first China GT race in Shanghai on Saturday, 5 September 2026.
- Millroy sustained multiple fractures and a bruised lung and is receiving hospital treatment.
- Millroy's team, FIST Auto, said they will not participate in future China GT races until safety concerns are discussed with organisers.
- China GT organisers admitted shortcomings and pledged to review the accident and improve emergency response and medical services.
