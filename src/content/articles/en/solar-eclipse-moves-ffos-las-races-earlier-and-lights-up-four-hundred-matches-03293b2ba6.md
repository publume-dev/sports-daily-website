- Because of the solar eclipse, the British Horseracing Authority moved Ffos Las's Wednesday race meeting about two hours earlier, with the first race changed from 17:40 BST to 15:35 BST.
- Floodlights will be turned on throughout four Hundred cricket matches on Wednesday because of the solar eclipse.
- Kempton racecourse said its 19:00 and 19:30 races on Wednesday will not be disrupted because they will be run under floodlights.
- The report does not identify the four Hundred matches affected or specify any athletics scheduling changes, despite the headline mentioning athletics.
