- Zolani Tete, a former two-division world champion, died at age 38 after a shooting outside his home in Mdantsane, South Africa.
- The South African Department of Sport confirmed Tete's death on Friday night.
- Tete set the record for the fastest knockout in a title fight in boxing history, knocking out Siboniso Gonya with his first punch.
- Tete's four-year suspension for a positive performance-enhancing drug test ended weeks before his death.
