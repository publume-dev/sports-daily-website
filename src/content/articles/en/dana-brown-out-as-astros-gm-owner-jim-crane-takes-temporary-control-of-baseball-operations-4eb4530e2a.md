- The Houston Astros and general manager Dana Brown have mutually agreed to part ways, with owner Jim Crane overseeing baseball operations through the transition period.
- Brown's responsibilities will be divided among assistant general managers Charles Cook, Gavin Dickey and Connor Huff, and senior advisor Jeff Bagwell.
- The Astros are currently tied for first place in the AL West with a 65-66 record, one game under .500.
