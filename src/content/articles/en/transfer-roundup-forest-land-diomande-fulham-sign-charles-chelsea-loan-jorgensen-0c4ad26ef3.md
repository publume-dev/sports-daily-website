- Nottingham Forest signed Côte d’Ivoire defender Ousmane Diomande from Sporting on a four-year contract with an option for a further year; the fee is reported at £34.2m.
- Fulham signed midfielder Shea Charles from Southampton for a reported £30m — a record for a Northern Ireland international — on a five-year deal with a club option to extend by 12 months.
- Chelsea goalkeeper Filip Jorgensen joined Strasbourg on a season-long loan for the 2026-27 campaign.
- Diomande made 132 appearances for Sporting after joining in January 2023 and started both legs of their Champions League quarter-final against Arsenal last season.
- The transfer fees for Diomande and Charles are reported figures, not officially confirmed by the clubs.
