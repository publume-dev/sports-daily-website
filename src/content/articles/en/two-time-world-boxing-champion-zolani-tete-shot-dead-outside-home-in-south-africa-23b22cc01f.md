- Two-time world champion boxer Zolani Tete was shot dead outside his home in Mdantsane, Eastern Cape, South Africa, on Friday.
- Tete, aged 38, was shot by two armed suspects in balaclavas; a 27-year-old female passenger was also shot and hospitalized.
- Tete held the WBO bantamweight title from 2017 to 2019 and the IBF super-flyweight title in 2014, and set a Guinness World Record for the fastest knockout in a title fight during that period.
- His manager confirmed Tete had been training for a comeback fight scheduled for 29 November.

The motive for the shooting remains unclear, and police have launched an investigation.
