- The Dallas Wings are 2-7 since the All-Star break and sit eighth place at 20-16 after a 78-70 loss to the Golden State Valkyries.
- Azzi Fudd has been sidelined since Aug. 5 with a right knee injury and has missed the last five games.
- The Wings are 1-5 without Fudd this season compared to 19-11 with her.
- Paige Bueckers' scoring average dropped from 20.9 to 19.3 points per game after the All-Star break, with her 3-point shooting falling from 39.7% to 32.5%.
- The Wings have an 0-7 record against top-eight opponents since the All-Star break and are 6-15 against playoff teams overall.
- Dallas starts a six-game homestand on Thursday against the Indiana Fever, followed by only lottery opponents for the rest of the regular season.

Fudd's availability for Thursday's game against the Fever is unclear, which could affect the Wings' performance in a critical stretch.
