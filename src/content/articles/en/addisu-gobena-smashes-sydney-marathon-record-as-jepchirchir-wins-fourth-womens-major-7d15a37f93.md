- Ethiopia's Addisu Gobena won the men's race in a course-record 2:04:42, breaking the previous mark of 2:06:06.
- Kenya's Peres Jepchirchir won the women's race in 2:18:31, her fourth major marathon title.
- The Sydney Marathon attracted 40,000 competitors this year.
- Daniel Romanchuk of the United States won the men's wheelchair race in a course-record 1:26:50.
- Switzerland's Manuela Schär won the women's wheelchair race in a course-record 1:42:04.
