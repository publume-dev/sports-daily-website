- Falcons edge rusher James Pearce Jr. was suspended eight games for violating the personal conduct policy, eligible for reinstatement on Nov. 2.
- Bills defensive tackle Phidarian Mathis was suspended three games for violating the substance abuse policy, eligible for reinstatement on Sept. 28.
- Titans cornerback Nazeeh Johnson was suspended six games for violating the performance-enhancing substances policy, eligible for reinstatement on Oct. 19.
- Commanders edge rusher Dorance Armstrong Jr. was suspended one game for violating the personal conduct policy.
