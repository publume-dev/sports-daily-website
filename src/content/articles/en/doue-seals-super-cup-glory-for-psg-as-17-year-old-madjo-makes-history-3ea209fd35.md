- Paris Saint-Germain defeated Aston Villa 2-1, with goals from Khvicha Kvaratskhelia and Désiré Doué; Brian Madjo scored for Villa.
- Madjo, aged 17, became the youngest scorer in UEFA Super Cup history.
- Marquinhos won his 40th trophy with PSG following the Super Cup victory.
- Madjo's registration was cleared by the Court of Arbitration for Sport just eight days before the match.
