- Ollie Lawrence and Elliot Daly have lost their England central contracts as Steve Borthwick omits them from his 25-man squad.
- Alex Coles and Asher Opoku-Fordjour are among the new inclusions, while Benhard van Rensburg and Courtney Lawes are overlooked.
- Five changes were made to the group, with Seb Atkinson, Guy Pepper, and Jack van Poortvliet receiving contracts, while Ben Curry, Tom Roebuck, and Tom Willis were omitted.
