- Safety Coby Bryant will miss four to six months after undergoing knee surgery, coach Ben Johnson announced Tuesday.
- Bryant suffered a hyperextended knee, bone bruise and small fracture during a training camp practice.
- The Bears are hopeful Bryant will be available in the playoffs should they clinch a spot.
- Chicago opens the 2026 season Sept. 13 against the Panthers.
